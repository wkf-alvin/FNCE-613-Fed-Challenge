05 · The decision test

One more step, without promising a cycle.

The evidence supports a small increase; the risks argue against signaling a sequence. Choose a path to see how the target range and recommendation change.

Supports a hike

Inflation is above target. Domestic demand is resilient. Two FOMC members preferred a hike in October.

Argues for caution

Policy works with a lag. Some inflation is supply-driven. September real PCE was roughly flat.

What would make us pause?

  • Clear evidence of softer demand.
  • Broader deterioration in labor conditions.
  • Faster disinflation in core measures.

One marginal increase, then reassess. The recommendation is a classroom position, not a forecast of the FOMC’s next move.

Recommended path: +25 bp, then pause if the data turn.
